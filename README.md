# Task 1: Biological Dataset Selection

## 📋 Project Overview
This repository contains the documentation and dataset selection for **Task 1** of the SWYNEX Bioinformatics Internship. The objective of this task is to select a public biological/genomic dataset, define its key characteristics, and outline a research question for downstream computational analysis.

---

## 🧬 Selected Dataset Details

* **Dataset Name:** Human Insulin Protein (`INS`)
* **UniProt Accession ID:** `P01308`
* **Repository / Source:** [UniProtKB (UniProt Knowledgebase)](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P01308/entry)
* **Organism:** *Homo sapiens* (Human)
* **Gene Name:** `INS`

---

## 📊 Dataset Size & Structural Scope

* **Protein Length:** 110 amino acids (Canonical sequence isoform `P01308-1`)
* **Molecular Weight:** 11,981 Da
* **Key Annotations & Features:**
  * **Natural Variants & Disease Mutations:** Includes 180 curated variants linked to disorders such as Hyperproinsulinemia, Type 2/Neonatal Diabetes Mellitus (PNDM4), and Maturity-Onset Diabetes of the Young (MODY10).
  * **Functional Domains:** Cleaved into the Insulin B chain (positions 25–54), C-peptide (positions 57–87), and Insulin A chain (positions 90–110), stabilized by three critical disulfide bonds.
  * **Protein-Protein Interactions:** Supported by binary interaction data with the Insulin Receptor (`INSR`), Insulin-Degrading Enzyme (`IDE`), and other key metabolic regulators.

---

## 🔬 Research Question

* **Primary Question:** *How do natural structural variants and amino acid mutations across the human insulin protein impact its binding affinity to the insulin receptor and post-translational processing pathways linked to diabetes mellitus?*
* **Internship Objective:** Analyzing this sequence-to-function relationship provides foundational insights into genetic variations, molecular disease mechanisms, and computational structural bioinformatics.
